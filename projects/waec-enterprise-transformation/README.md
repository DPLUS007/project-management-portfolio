# Sidmach / WAEC — ERP, COTS & Enterprise Technology Transformation

**Role:** Project Manager  
**Specialisms:** ERP implementation coordination, COTS solutions, Oracle Fusion Cloud ERP, infrastructure, vendor and stakeholder management

## Executive summary
I coordinated enterprise technology delivery involving Sidmach and the West African Examinations Council (WAEC), including **ERP and COTS (Commercial Off-the-Shelf) solution delivery** and related infrastructure/application dependencies. The environment included **30+ standalone servers and related applications/infrastructure in scope**. The wider program context included a **reported 98% reduction in examination-malpractice incidents**; this is a reported outcome, not a claim of independently verified personal attribution.

## Business challenge
Enterprise modernization required coordinating business requirements, packaged applications, vendors, technical environments, integration dependencies and stakeholder readiness. ERP/COTS implementations create important fit-gap questions: when can the business adopt standard functionality, when is configuration appropriate, and when does a gap require a separate decision?

## My project-management contribution
- Coordinated ERP/COTS delivery plans, scope, workstreams, milestones and dependencies.
- Supported business-requirements gathering, process mapping and fit-gap conversations.
- Coordinated discussions between business owners, ERP/COTS vendors, infrastructure specialists and technical delivery teams.
- Tracked configuration-related decisions, integration dependencies and unresolved assumptions.
- Supported environment, data, access and infrastructure readiness planning.
- Maintained risks, assumptions, issues and dependencies (RAID) with owners and escalation paths.
- Coordinated testing, defect follow-up, user acceptance testing (UAT) and implementation readiness.
- Supported stakeholder communication, deployment/cutover planning and operational handover considerations.

## ERP and COTS implementation lifecycle
### 1. Discovery and requirements
Identify business processes, stakeholders, functional/non-functional requirements, reporting needs and dependencies.

### 2. Fit-gap and solution decisions
Work with solution specialists to compare standard COTS/ERP functionality with business needs. Record configuration, process change, integration or exception decisions and their delivery implications.

### 3. Delivery planning and vendor coordination
Translate solution decisions into milestones, ownership, dependencies, risks and deliverables. Track vendor commitments and cross-team handoffs.

### 4. Integration and environment readiness
Coordinate dependencies across servers, databases, identity/access, networks, interfaces, data and supporting applications.

### 5. Testing and UAT
Support test scenarios, defect triage, business validation, issue resolution and go/no-go readiness discussions.

### 6. Deployment and transition
Coordinate cutover readiness, stakeholder communications, training/change considerations, support ownership and handover documentation.
