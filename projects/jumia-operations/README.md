# Jumia — E-commerce, Fulfillment & Logistics Coordination

**Role:** Project Coordinator  
**Scope:** 15+ concurrent workstreams

## Overview
Supported cross-functional operational coordination across marketplace, inventory, warehousing, fulfillment, technology, customer service and last-mile logistics.

## Responsibilities
- Maintained visibility across **15+ concurrent workstreams**.
- Tracked actions, blockers, owners and operational dependencies.

Strengthened cross-functional communication and operational coordination in a fast-paced, customer-facing environment.

**Competencies:** Project coordination, e-commerce operations, logistics, inventory, reporting, dependency management.
