# Project Blue World — Environmental Stakeholder Coordination

**Role:** Volunteer Project Manager

## Overview
Supported mission-driven environmental initiatives where progress depended on alignment among partners and participants.

## Responsibilities
- Coordinated meetings and stakeholder discussions.
- Tracked actions, decisions, owners and follow-up.
- Supported partner engagement and communication.
- Helped identify unresolved dependencies and maintain project momentum.

## Delivery approach
Used structured agendas, documented decisions, action ownership and consistent follow-up to maintain accountability without relying on formal authority.

## Competencies
Stakeholder engagement, facilitation, project coordination, decision tracking, communication and collaborative planning.
