# Meet Robin — AI Product Delivery & Quality Evaluation

**Role:** Project Manager (Co-op)  
**Scope:** Three-person team, four-month program, six milestones

## Overview
Supported an AI product team by turning evolving product goals into structured requirements, backlog items, milestone plans, acceptance criteria and evaluation practices.

## Responsibilities
- Coordinated six milestones across a three-person team.
- Clarified requirements, assumptions, acceptance criteria and deliverables.
- Supported scenario-based evaluation of AI/LLM outputs.
- Documented issues, decisions and quality expectations.
- Created **five reusable documentation templates** to improve consistency.

## Delivery approach
Linked requirements to observable acceptance criteria; planned short feedback cycles; treated evaluation as part of delivery rather than an end-stage activity; and captured reusable knowledge.

## Outcome
Improved clarity and traceability of product expectations, milestones and quality evaluation in an experimental technology environment.

**Competencies:** AI delivery, backlog coordination, product requirements, acceptance criteria, evaluation, documentation, stakeholder communication.
