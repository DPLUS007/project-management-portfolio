# Stakeholder Action & Decision Log

Use this template to keep stakeholder commitments, decisions and follow-up visible across project workstreams.

## Stakeholder actions

| ID | Stakeholder / Team | Action | Owner | Due Date | Priority | Status | Dependency / Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

## Decisions

| Decision ID | Decision Required | Decision Owner | Required By | Outcome | Impact / Follow-up |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

## Escalations

| Item | Reason for Escalation | Escalated To | Date | Required Action | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

## Working practice

- Assign one accountable owner to every action.
- Record dates and dependencies rather than relying on meeting notes alone.
- Surface overdue or high-impact actions in project status reporting.
- Capture decisions with enough context to support later traceability.
- Link significant blockers to the RAID log where appropriate.

> Illustrative project-management template; no confidential employer or client information is included.
