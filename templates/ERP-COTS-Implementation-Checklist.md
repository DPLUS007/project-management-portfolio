# ERP / COTS Implementation Checklist

## Discovery
- [ ] Identify sponsor, business owner and delivery team
- [ ] Document current processes and target outcomes
- [ ] Capture functional and non-functional requirements
- [ ] Identify data, reporting, security and integration needs

## Solution fit and vendors
- [ ] Document fit-gap decisions
- [ ] Assign owners for configuration, process changes and integrations
- [ ] Track vendor deliverables, assumptions and constraints
- [ ] Approve scope and change-control approach

## Delivery and readiness
- [ ] Confirm environments, access, infrastructure and data dependencies
- [ ] Maintain RAID log and milestone plan
- [ ] Agree testing approach, acceptance criteria and UAT owners
- [ ] Track defects and retesting
- [ ] Confirm training, communications and business readiness
- [ ] Review cutover, rollback and go/no-go criteria
- [ ] Confirm hypercare and support handover
