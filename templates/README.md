# Reusable Project Delivery Templates

These practical templates illustrate the structures used to coordinate projects, including **ERP/COTS implementations, enterprise technology delivery, stakeholder governance, and AI product initiatives**.

| Template | Purpose |
|---|---|
| [ERP/COTS Implementation Checklist](ERP-COTS-Implementation-Checklist.md) | Requirements, fit-gap, vendor coordination, testing, UAT, cutover and handover readiness |
| [RAID Log](RAID-Log.md) | Risks, assumptions, issues and dependencies with ownership and response actions |
| [Project Status Report](Project-Status-Report.md) | Executive status, milestones, risks/issues and decisions required |
| [Requirements & Acceptance Criteria](Requirements-and-Acceptance-Criteria.md) | Business requirements, ERP/COTS fit-gap classification, dependencies and validation |
| [Stakeholder Action & Decision Log](Stakeholder-Action-Log.md) | Stakeholder commitments, decisions, escalations and follow-up |
| [AI / LLM Evaluation Checklist](AI-Evaluation-Checklist.md) | Scenario-based AI quality evaluation, defect iteration and release readiness |

## How these artifacts support delivery

The templates are designed to connect project governance with execution. For ERP/COTS work, requirements and fit-gap decisions flow into dependencies, RAID management, UAT and deployment readiness. For AI/product work, acceptance criteria and evaluation scenarios make quality expectations observable and traceable. Stakeholder actions and status reporting keep ownership, decisions and escalation visible throughout delivery.

> These are illustrative portfolio templates created to demonstrate delivery practices. They do not contain confidential employer, client or proprietary project information.
